# What works, and what doesn't

Honest status, not a feature list. If something below sounds like it should work and doesn't
for you, that's exactly what [a log](troubleshooting.html) is for.

## Working and in regular use

- Lap times, with a sector-by-sector breakdown
- Live delta to your best
- Fuel use and warnings
- Off-track and incident calls
- Flags
- Gaps to the cars around you
- Calls on passes as they happen
- The full pit sequence, limiter to release
- Voice questions - see [Voice commands](voice-commands.html)

## Rough or unproven

- **Le Mans Ultimate** support is partial. Some things are wired, plenty aren't - it's actively
  being worked on and tested live.
- **Blue flags** were genuinely broken until 0.2.0-alpha - Gary was reading a field iRacing
  never puts them in. That's fixed, but blue flags in general only get tested when someone
  actually gets lapped, so treat it as improving-but-unproven rather than known-good. If you get
  blue-flagged and hear nothing, send a log.
- **Rally and dirt** handling exists but has barely been driven.
- Pit stop time estimates need a few stops at a given car and track before Gary will quote one.
  He'll tell you when he hasn't got enough to go on. This gets better as more testers pit -
  see [Pit data & privacy](pit-data.html) for how that works.

## Why this page exists

Gary is built by watching real telemetry live and fixing what's actually wrong, not by guessing
what a game's data probably means. That means some things are verified solid, some are "should
work, not proven yet," and being upfront about which is which is more useful than a feature list
that doesn't say.
