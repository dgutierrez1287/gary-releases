# Le Mans Ultimate

Everything under [In both sims](./#in-both-sims), plus:

## Fuel and Virtual Energy

Gary works with LMU's pit menu directly. With automatic fuelling on, he sets fuel - or Virtual
Energy for the Hypercars and GT3s - for the end of the race plus your margin every time you
turn into the pits, and tells you what he set. *"Fuel to the end"*, *"add 5 laps of energy"*
and the rest work by voice too. *"How's my energy"* and *"fuel to energy balance"* tell you
where you stand.

## Penalties

Gary knows whether you've got a **stop-and-go** or a **drive-through**, and tells you which. The
reminders to serve it come in the last sector, and the box countdown only happens when you
actually have to stop.

## Track limits

Your track limit points are read out when they change, and you get a warning when you're one
step off a penalty. Ask *"how many track limit points do I have"* any time. The grid briefing
covers the track limit rule for the race.

## Tyres

- **Wear and temperatures**, live - warnings when they're worn, or too cold or hot (you set the
  thresholds), and on request.
- **Tyre allowance** in the grid briefing - how many you have for the race.
- *"How many new tyres have I got"*, *"what used tyres do I have"*, *"what tyres am I on"*. Just
  *"how many tyres have I got left"* gets *"New or used?"*, and *"how are my tyres"* gets *"Wear,
  or temperatures?"* - answer with one word.

## Hybrid (Hypercars)

Gary keeps an eye on the battery and speaks up when it's draining, building or sitting full - and
**tells you which click to make**. Draining: *"take regen up a click, to 153 kilowatts"*, or once
regen is maxed, *"take deploy down a click"*. Building: deploy up, then regen down. He knows where
both settings are and how far each can go, so he never suggests a click that isn't there.

**He follows your regen setting.** LMU doesn't publish it while you drive, so Gary reads which
buttons or keys LMU has bound to regen up and down - wheel, button box, keyboard or a Stream Deck
hotkey - and counts your presses, checking the count every lap against how hard the battery
charged. Bind regen in LMU's controls and it just works.

**He learns what works.** Every lap on one deploy and regen setting is remembered per car and
track. After a few laps, the battery calls use that to say how many clicks, and a new session can
open with *"from your laps here, deploy 40 with regen 170 held the battery about level"*. In a race
he aims to finish with about 15 percent. *"How did the battery go here last time"* still gives
you the last race.

*"How's my fuel ratio"* tells you the FUEL RATIO that's set, what your driving actually uses, and
what to set at the next stop.

## Damage

Punctures and lost wheels by corner, dents by area of the car - front, rear, each side and each
corner - and suspension damage, called when it happens and on request.

## Sectors

Your sector times against your best, called at the end of the lap or live - and
*"what's my sector time"* on request.
