# Gary
{: .fs-9 }

A crew chief for iRacing and Le Mans Ultimate. He watches your telemetry and tells you the
things you'd otherwise be reading off an overlay — lap times and where you found or lost them,
fuel, gaps, flags, incidents, and the whole pit sequence from the limiter to "go, go, go".

## What Gary does

He's a crew chief and a spotter on your radio. The idea is that you can turn off most of your
overlays: anything you'd glance at one for, Gary either tells you when it matters or you can
just ask him. Hold push-to-talk and say *"what's my delta"*, *"how's my fuel"*, *"gap to the car
behind"* - he's forgiving about how you phrase it.

Without being asked, he calls your lap times and where you found or lost time, fuel, gaps,
flags, penalties, traffic from other classes, and the whole pit sequence - and he can set your
fuel for you at every stop. Each sim gets its own extras too: shared pit and fuel data, the
two-compound rule and oval tyre allowances in iRacing; stop-and-go vs drive-through, track
limit points and the Hypercar battery in LMU.

**[See all the features →](features/)**

{: .note }
**Gary is in alpha.** It's free, it's in daily use, and it's still changing fast. If he gets
something wrong, send a log in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/XAdbGx8zV8) - that's how most
things get fixed.
