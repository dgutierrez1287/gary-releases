# Gary
{: .fs-9 }

A crew chief for iRacing and Le Mans Ultimate. He watches your telemetry and tells you the
things you'd otherwise be reading off an overlay — lap times and where you found or lost them,
fuel, gaps, flags, incidents, and the whole pit sequence from the limiter to "go, go, go".
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

[Download the latest release](https://github.com/dgutierrez1287/gary-releases/releases/latest){: .btn .btn-primary .fs-5 .mb-4 .mb-md-0 .mr-2 }
[Install instructions](install.html){: .btn .fs-5 .mb-4 .mb-md-0 }

---

## What Gary does

Hold your push-to-talk button and just ask him things — *"what's my delta"*, *"fuel usage"*,
*"what's my last lap"* — and he answers, forgiving about how you phrase it. He also talks
without being asked: lap times with a sector-by-sector breakdown, live delta, fuel warnings,
off-track and incident calls, flags, gaps to the cars around you, calls on passes as they
happen, and the full pit sequence.

{: .note }
**This is an alpha.** It works, it's in daily use, and it will still get things wrong. See
[What works, and what doesn't](whats-working.html) for the honest state of things.
